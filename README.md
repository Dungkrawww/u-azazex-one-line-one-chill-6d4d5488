Entry for the make.fun competition "One Line, One Chill" ($5 in USDC).

The entry line (97 characters):

> The house settled after midnight, and every door I had locked clicked open, one by one, in order.

Why it lands: it frightens by what it withholds — no monster is named, no gore is shown. The dread is earned from concrete detail (locked doors, the click, one by one) and the last two words land the blow: the doors did not open at random, they opened *in order*, which means something is choosing them.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
